How we work

We sell crushed rock and gravel materials, plus sand and soil products, subject to availability, loaded directly at our pit in Märsta. Both businesses and private customers are welcome.

  • Bring a trailer and pick up yourself — pay on site by card or cash.
  • Need help loading material onto your trailer? We can help for a small fee.
  • Want it delivered? We can arrange transport — contact us for a quote.
  • The pit is open Mon–Fri 06:00–15:00, plus bridge days.
