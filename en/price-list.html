Our price lists

Below are our official price lists for crushed rock, gravel and winter materials. Our price lists are published in Swedish only — the pricing itself (material, size, SEK per tonne) is straightforward even if you don’t read Swedish, and we’re always happy to help over the phone or by email. In addition to the material itself, we also offer truck delivery, track spreading and road spreading — contact us for a quote and pricing.

All prices above exclude VAT (25%). On top of the listed prices, an environmental fee, a seasonal (winter) surcharge, and a natural gravel tax (on gravel materials) may apply — see each price list for exact amounts.

Cars with trailers: payment is taken on site by card or cash. If you need help loading material onto your trailer, a fee of 250 SEK excl. material applies.
